Privacy

Solarna PV privacy notice.

Last updated: 20 September 2026.

Data used

Solarna processes the site coordinates, project name, PV-system settings and analysis options that you submit. If you import inverter production, Solarna also processes the dates and energy values in that file. Completed reports contain the submitted inputs and calculated results.

Purpose

The data is used only to retrieve solar-weather information, calculate PV orientation and energy estimates, and display or save your results.

Where data is processed

Site coordinates may be sent to the European Commission Joint Research Centre PVGIS service. Account projects and imported production are stored in the configured Supabase project and restricted to the signed-in user. The Solarna server also retains operational report files. Hosting providers may process routine network information needed to deliver and secure the service.

On your device

The application stores the server address, login session, form draft and last displayed report on your device. It does not store the password. Clearing the application or website data removes that information.

No tracking

Solarna PV has no advertising, cross-app tracking, data brokerage or sale of personal data.

Retention and requests

Account projects and production data remain until they are deleted. Operational reports remain on the configured Solarna server until its operator removes them. To request access, correction or deletion, email contact@solarnasystems.com.
